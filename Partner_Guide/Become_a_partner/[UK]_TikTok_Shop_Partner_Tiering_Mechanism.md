# [UK] TikTok Shop Partner Tiering Mechanism

The TikTok Shop Partners('TSP') tiering system is designed to cultivate a robust ecosystem, set service benchmarks, and offer customized resources for TSP at various development stages—thereby guiding their growth path. 
In the tier system, TSP will be referred to the integration of all TSP/TAP/CAP. 
*This Tiering Mechanism only applies to TikTok Shop Partners certified and operating in the UK market.*

# **Rules for TSP Tiering** 
## **TSP Tiers**
TSPs will be divided into 5 tiers from S1 to S5, corresponding to：
**S1 TSP->S2 Bronze TSP->S3 Silver TSP->S4 Gold TSP->S5 Platinum TSP**
The higher the partner's rating, the stronger its comprehensive promotion capabilities.

# **TSP Tier Update Schedule and Announcement Mechanism**
## **Update Schedule**

* The TSP tier will be updated on the 3rd~5th day of each following month, and it will be calculated according to the performance data of the partner in the previous month. 
* There may be a delay in updating the tiering results, and the actual update will be based on the notification within the product. 
* The tiering result is automatically calculated by the system based on multiple performance indicators. It may also be partially adjusted manually based on the agency’s actual servicing of sellers/creators. Appeals are not supported at this stage.

# **Governance of TSP** 
## General Rules for Seller & Creator Servive

* During the period of agency's cooperation, serious violations occurred in e-commerce transactions and content, including but not limited to unauthorised streaming/recorded broadcasts/fake orders/illegal transactions, etc, will cause the termination of TSP on the platform. 

## For Seller Service:
### Merchant Binding

* After onboarding, agencies with no merchant binding for over 3 months will receive a warning email.
* After onboarding, agencies with no merchant binding for over 6 months will be removed.

### Client Complaint

* Agencies receive more than 3 times client complaint in 1 month will receive a warning email.
* After a formal warning has been issued with no subsequent improvement, and upon receiving 5 additional consecutive complaints, the partnership will be terminated.

## For Creator Service:

* Monitoring：There is a regular monitor of agencies for policy violations (e.g. pirated or low-quality content).
* Warning：If risks are identified, agencies may receive a warning and enter a **7-day observation period** to fix issues.
* Enforcement：If no improvement or further violations occur, actions may be taken, including **partnership removal.**
* Appeal：Agencies can submit appeals, which will be reviewed within **48 hours.**

#### ++*What happened after the removal process of TSP++

1. General Rules:
* Qualification removal: Removal of qualification in a specific country is irreversible. Can only add on qualifications in other countries;
* Unable to add new service categories;
* Services that have already been published under enabled categories will be taken down;
* The corresponding service category menu in TTSPC will disappear;
* Partners will be removed from the ranking list. 
2. Creator Service Specific: 
* Partners will automatically unbind from creators that were previously linked. 
3. Seller Service Specific: 
* Services published under the removed qualification will be marked as taken down;
* Service subscriptions will be canceled.
