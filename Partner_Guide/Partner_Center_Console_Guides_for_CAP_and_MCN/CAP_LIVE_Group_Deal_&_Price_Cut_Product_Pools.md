# CAP LIVE Group Deal & Price Cut Product Pools
## What are LIVE Products?
LIVE Products allows Creator Agency Partners (CAPs) to discover, organize, and share products available for different TikTok Shop LIVE promotional campaigns.
Partners can explore three types of LIVE promotions: **Flash Sale, Group Buy, and Slash Buy**, and create dedicated Product Sets to share with their linked creators.
## How It Works?

* Browse products available for Flash Sale, Group Buy, and Slash Buy campaigns in one place.
* Search for products by product name, product ID, shop code, or shop name.
* Filter products by campaign time.
* View promotional details, including pricing, inventory, and participation requirements.
* Create separate Product Sets for each LIVE promotion type.
* Share Product Sets with linked creators, allowing them to select products and add them to their showcase.

## What Types of LIVE Products Are Available?
| **Promotion Type** | **Description** | **Product Information** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flash Sale** | Existing LIVE Flash Sale product selection experience. | Existing product information |
| **Group Buy** | Products available through public LIVE group-purchase campaigns. | Original price, discounted price, required group size, inventory |
| **Slash Buy** | Products available through public LIVE price-reduction campaigns. | Original price, discounted price, required participant count, inventory |
**Note:** Group Buy and Slash Buy product listings include products from LIVE campaigns configured as publicly available to creators.
## How to Use LIVE Products?
### Step 1 – Navigate to LIVE Products

1. Log in to your **TikTok Shop Partner Center** account.
2. Go to **Product Management → Product Marketplace**.
3. Click **Live Products**.

The Live Products page includes three tabs:

* **Flash Sale**
* **Group deal**
* **Price cut**

Select the promotion type you want to explore.

* Original price

### Step 4 – Create a Product Set
Product Sets allow you to organize selected LIVE products and share them with linked creators.

1. Select the **Flash Sale**, **Group Deal**, or **Price cut** tab.
2. Click **Product sets** in the upper-right corner.
3. Click **Create product set**.
4. Complete the following information:
   * **Target Market:** Automatically defaults to your current market.
   * **Product set name:** Required.
   * **Product set description:** Optional.
5. Click **Create**.

Your new Product Set will appear in the Product Sets list.
**Note:** Each LIVE promotion type has its own Product Sets. A Product Set created under Group Buy is separate from Product Sets created under Flash Sale or Slash Buy.
### Step 5 – Add Products to a Product Set

1. Return to the applicable LIVE product marketplace tab.
2. Find the product you want to add.
3. Click **Add** on the product card.
4. Select the Product Set you want to add the product to.
5. Click **Close** to complete the action.

You can open a Product Set from the Product Sets list to review its products.
### Step 6 – Share a Product Set With Creators
Once your Product Set is ready:

1. Navigate to the corresponding **Product sets** page.
2. Locate the Product Set you want to share.
3. Click **Share**.
4. Share the Product Set link with your linked creators using the existing sharing functionality.

### Step 7 – Creator Selects Products
When a creator receives your shared Product Set link:

1. The creator opens the link to view the shared products.
2. The landing page identifies the relevant LIVE promotion type.
3. The creator reviews the available products.
4. The creator clicks **Add to showcase** to add selected products to their showcase.

**Note:** The final creator-side page design and interaction details are subject to confirmation.
## FAQ
**Q1: Can I create one Product Set for Flash Sale, Group Buy, and Slash Buy products?**
No. Each LIVE promotion type has its own Product Sets. Create and manage Product Sets separately under the corresponding promotion tab.
**Q2: Can I share Product Sets with creators?**
Yes. CAPs can share Product Sets with their linked creators using the existing Product Set sharing functionality.
**Q3: Which Group Buy and Slash Buy products are displayed?**
The marketplace displays eligible products from LIVE campaigns configured as publicly available to creators.
**Q4: Can I search for a specific LIVE product?**
Yes. You can search by product name, product ID, shop code, or shop name. Campaign time filtering is also supported for Group Buy and Slash Buy.
**Q5: Will existing Flash Sale Product Sets be affected?**
The existing Flash Sale product selection and sharing functionality will be retained. The navigation will be organized under the new **Live Products** section.
**Q6: Will my existing Open Products Product Sets change?**
No. The existing Open Products Product Sets functionality remains unchanged.
**Q7: How can creators add products from a shared Product Set?**
Creators can open the shared Product Set link, select products, and click **Add to showcase** to add them to their showcase.
